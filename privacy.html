Privacy Policy

This application is used by one person only (its developer) to read his or her own Google Calendar events. The data accessed (calendar events) is stored and used only inside the developer's own Home Assistant installation on a private home network. It is not shared with, sold to, or disclosed to any third party.

Use of Google user data is limited to this purpose and complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
